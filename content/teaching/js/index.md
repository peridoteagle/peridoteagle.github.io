Data science offers powerful tools for addressing societal challenges, but it is not a magic solution. In this course, students examine where data science can genuinely help, where it can fall short, and what responsible practice looks like in real projects.

## Institution

North Carolina State University

I have taught this course for eight semesters (Fall 2022 - Present) as a part-time lecturer with the North Carolina State University (NCSU) Data Science Academy (DSA).

## Format

This course is a discussion-oriented, 1-credit virtual class. Students engage with examples from current practice and reflect on how technical decisions connect to real human outcomes.
